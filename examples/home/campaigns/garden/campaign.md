## What this is

A stand-in campaign under the invented `home` estate. Replace this
file's frontmatter and body with your own: `paths` lists extra
directories the agent may work in, and the `claude:` table passes flags
straight through to the claude adapter (`remote-control: true` becomes
a bare `--remote-control`).
